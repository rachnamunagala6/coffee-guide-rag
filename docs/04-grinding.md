# Grinding Coffee

## Why grinding matters
Grind size controls how quickly water extracts flavor from the coffee. Finer grounds extract faster; coarser grounds extract slower. The right size depends on how long the water is in contact with the coffee in your brew method. An even grind, with particles of similar size, gives a cleaner and more balanced cup.

## Burr grinder versus blade grinder
- **Burr grinders** crush beans between two burrs set a fixed distance apart, producing an even grind with adjustable size. This is the biggest single upgrade most home brewers can make. Manual (hand-crank) burr grinders give excellent results for a lower price than electric ones.
- **Blade grinders** chop beans with a spinning blade, producing a mix of dust and chunks. If you use one, pulse in short bursts and shake the grinder between pulses for a more even result. Grind longer for finer, shorter for coarser.

## Grind size by brew method
| Brew method | Grind size | Looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Turkish coffee | Extra fine | Flour or powdered sugar |
| Espresso | Fine | Fine table salt, slightly clumpy |
| Moka pot | Fine to medium-fine | Between table salt and fine sand |
| AeroPress | Medium-fine to fine | Table salt (depends on recipe) |
| Pour-over (V60, Kalita) | Medium-fine | Coarse sand or table salt |
| Drip coffee machine | Medium | Regular sand |
| Chemex | Medium-coarse | Rough sand |
| French press | Coarse | Sea salt or breadcrumbs |
| Cold brew | Extra coarse | Peppercorns or coarse sea salt |

## Adjusting your grind
- Coffee tastes **sour, thin or weak**, or water runs through **too fast**: grind finer.
- Coffee tastes **bitter, harsh or dry**, or water drains **too slowly**: grind coarser.
Change one step at a time and change only one thing (grind, dose or water) between brews.

## Grinding tips
- Grind immediately before brewing.
- Weigh beans before grinding rather than grinding extra.
- Clean the grinder every few weeks by brushing out old grounds; oily dark roasts leave residue that tastes rancid over time.
